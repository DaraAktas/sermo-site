Your student community, in one app.

Sermo connects University of Manchester students across every faculty: find a mentor, ask anything, keep up with societies and events, and never miss a deadline.

Coming soon to the App Store

Mentoring

First-year students are matched with a second-year student mentor, from their own course or field first, and second-years can volunteer to mentor.

Ask without your name

Post questions and polls without your name shown to other students, and answer theirs. Every post can be reported, and its author blocked, from the post itself.

Events and societies

Follow societies, RSVP to events and check in with a QR code.

Opportunities

Find internships, placements, spring weeks, graduate schemes, vacation schemes and scholarships for your subject, and track your applications.

Modules

Add the modules on your course and ask questions about each one.

Chat

Message your matched mentor, with blocking and reporting built in.

Sermo is for current University of Manchester students aged 18 and over. Sign-up needs an @manchester.ac.uk email address. Questions? Email support@sermocampus.com.
